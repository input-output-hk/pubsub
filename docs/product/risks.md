# Risks & Asks

## Risks

| Risk | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Technical Complexity** | A ground-up build of a new native network protocol and DA layer carries significant engineering challenges and timeline risks |
| **Protocol Dependencies** | The project's success depends on protocol-level changes (e.g., Plutus events) being prioritized and included in the Leios hard fork |
| **Adoption Inertia** | Key wallets and dApps across both ecosystems may be slow to integrate the unified solution |
| **Vendor Delivery** | Reliance on external vendors for core development introduces potential delays and quality control challenges |

---

## Asks

### Engineering
Prioritized resourcing for a 3-month rapid development phase.

### Protocol-Level Support
Executive commitment to ensure necessary CIPs/MIPs (e.g., Plutus Events) are included in the Leios roadmap.

### Partnerships
Immediate engagement with the Hyperledger Identus team to formalize partnership and technical integration.

### Vendor Management
A clear process for selecting and managing multiple development vendors to build out the required components.

## Marketing & Comms

A community-focused strategy will begin immediately to iterate on feedback and attract talent.

### Phase 1 (Immediate)
Create a public Discord server for vendors, engineers, and community members to discuss the project, get feedback, and iterate on the value proposition.

### Phase 2 (Beacon Launch)
Announce the unified vision and roadmap via technical blog posts. Showcase prototypes to demonstrate progress and functionality.

### Phase 3 (Agora Launch)
Broader community announcements highlighting the decentralized Agora network, the token launch, and tutorials for developers and SPOs.
