# Vertical 2: Social & Community

**Building the Cardano Fediverse**

This vertical leverages Cardano PubSub's unique features for on-chain permissioning and decentralized moderation to build a rich, community-governed social ecosystem on Cardano.

## Use Cases

### Token-Gated Social Networks

Creation of decentralized social feeds and chat groups where access is controlled by on-chain conditions, such as holding a specific NFT or native token.

**Core Benefit:** Adds significant utility to existing tokens/NFTs and enables the creation of powerful, exclusive, and self-governing communities.

---

### Decentralized Moderation

A system where users and dApps can subscribe to moderation lists curated by trusted "Moderation DAOs" for scalable, client-side filtering of spam and malicious content.

**Core Benefit:** Solves a key challenge for open networks by creating a market-driven, decentralized economy for community safety and trust.

---

### ActivityPub Bridge

A specialized bridge service that connects Cardano to the wider decentralized social web (the "Fediverse") by translating public Cardano PubSub messages into the standard ActivityPub format.

**Core Benefit:** Dramatically expands Cardano's reach and interoperability, allowing on-chain events to be visible and interactive on external platforms like Mastodon.
