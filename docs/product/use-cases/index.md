# Use Cases

Cardano PubSub use cases are organized into five strategic verticals:

1. **[Foundational](foundational.md)** — Securing & Enhancing the Core Experience
2. **[Social & Community](social.md)** — Building the Cardano Fediverse
3. **[Commerce & Services](commerce.md)** — The In-Wallet "dApplet" Storefront
4. **[AI & Agentic Systems](ai-agents.md)** — The Autonomous Economy
5. **[Ecosystem Expansion](ecosystem.md)** — Cross-Chain Growth

## Overview

Each vertical addresses different stakeholder needs and market opportunities:

| Vertical | Primary Focus | Key Stakeholders |
|----------|---------------|------------------|
| Foundational | DeFi alerts, governance, SPO communication | Liqwid, DReps, SPOs |
| Social & Community | Token-gated networks, decentralized moderation | Community builders, DAOs |
| Commerce & Services | In-wallet marketplace, dApplets | Brands, developers |
| AI & Agentic Systems | Autonomous agents, machine-to-machine | DeFi protocols, DAOs |
| Ecosystem Expansion | Cross-chain governance, bridge & stake | Minswap, external users |
