# Vertical 1: Foundational

**Securing & Enhancing the Core Experience**

This vertical focuses on leveraging Cardano PubSub to solve the immediate communication challenges within the IOG ecosystem, making core on-chain interactions safer, more efficient, and more intuitive for all users.

## Use Cases

### Actionable DeFi Alerts

Transforms static notifications (e.g., liquidation warnings) into interactive messages. A user receiving an alert can take immediate, one-click action (e.g., "Repay 10%," "Add Collateral") from within their wallet.

**Core Benefit:** Drastically reduces reaction time to prevent financial loss and improves the core DeFi user experience and security. *A primary need identified by Liqwid.*

---

### Unified Governance Comms

Delivers proposal notifications for Cardano DAOs and Midnight governance actions with embedded "Vote Yes/No/Abstain" buttons, converting voting from a multi-step process into a single, secure action within the wallet.

**Core Benefit:** Lowers the barrier to participation, increases voter turnout, and strengthens decentralization across all ecosystems.

---

### DRep Communication

Allows Delegated Representatives (DReps) on Cardano to establish a direct, authenticated communication channel with the wallets that have delegated to them for updates, voting rationales, and calls to action.

**Core Benefit:** Fosters a more informed and engaged electorate for on-chain governance, increasing transparency and accountability. *A use case highlighted by C.H.*

---

### Targeted SPO Polling & Communication

Creates a dedicated, authenticated channel for governance bodies to broadcast polls, surveys, and critical updates directly to all registered SPOs. Operator tools can subscribe to this channel, providing automated notifications.

**Core Benefit:** Replaces inefficient communication via social media with a direct, trusted channel. Increases participation in crucial ecosystem surveys and ensures a representative sample of operator opinions.

---

### Multi-Sig Keychain Notifications

When a member of a multi-sig wallet signs a transaction, all other members receive an immediate notification informing them of the action and prompting them if their signature is now required to reach the threshold.

**Core Benefit:** Increases security and operational efficiency for teams and DAOs managing shared funds, preventing delays and unauthorized actions. *A use case highlighted by C.H.*

---

### SPO-Delegator Communication

Provides a direct, authenticated communication channel for Stake Pool Operators (SPOs) to engage with their delegators for announcements about maintenance, fee changes, or rewards.

**Core Benefit:** Fosters a stronger, more transparent community and improves the relationship between SPOs and their delegators. *A use case highlighted by C.H.*

---

### Secure dApp Update Channels

Allows developers to establish cryptographically-verified broadcast channels to their users' wallets for authentic updates, new features, or security warnings.

**Core Benefit:** Eliminates the risk of phishing and impersonation common on Web2 platforms, increasing user trust.

---

### Intelligent Subscriptions

A wallet user who interacts with a new dApp is automatically prompted if they would like to subscribe to that dApp's notification channel, creating a seamless and context-aware onboarding experience.

**Core Benefit:** Improves user engagement and makes discovery of relevant communication channels frictionless. *A use case highlighted by C.H.*
