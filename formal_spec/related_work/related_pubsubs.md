# Byzantine-Resilient, Brokerless Pub/Sub Protocols

Pubsub protocol inclusion criteria:

1. **Byzantine resilient** — designed with malicious node behavior in mind, not just crashes.
2. **Not broker based** — peer-to-peer / gossip dissemination, no dedicated broker overlay.
3. **Peer-reviewed publication** — appears in a reputable conference or journal.
4. **No centralized trusted authority** — correctness must not hinge on any *single designated party* being honest/altruistic at runtime (no trusted broadcaster, dealer, sequencer, safety-critical leader, or auditor). *Acceptable:* a **decentralized** membership/identity layer — our design registers peers on a **blockchain** (a known, Sybil-resisted peer set with no central authority) — and standard threshold / honest-majority (e.g. `n > 3f`) assumptions. *Excluded:* centralized runtime trust. (Sharpens #2: "brokerless" alone is not enough.)

**Two tables:** (1) **pub/sub & dissemination systems**; (2) **Byzantine reliable-broadcast primitives**.

## Byzantine reliable broadcast primitives

Full BRB is **overkill** — it adds agreement/non-equivocation + all-or-nothing-vs-Byzantine-sender we don't need; only the **validity / dissemination slice** matters.

| Protocol | Byzantine resilient? | Brokerless? | Peer-reviewed? | Relevance to us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dynamic Probabilistic Reliable Broadcast** (Anikina, Bezerra, Kuznetsov, Schiff, Schmid) | Yes — probabilistic BRB, `f<n/3`, resistant to a **slow adaptive adversary** via dynamically re-randomized **witness sets** (stream-local hashing) — *NOT* "under churn" (table corrected) | Yes — sampling/witness-based, no brokers | Yes — OPODIS 2024 | Full BRB is overkill; its adaptive-adversary defense is **coupled to a witness-set quorum we don't use** and doesn't transfer — only the general "beacon-reseed a decisive selection" *principle* survives (relevant only for adaptively-targetable sampling) |
| **Dynamic Byzantine Reliable Broadcast (DBRB)** (Guerraoui, Komatović, Kuznetsov, Pignolet, Seredinschi, Tonkikh) | Yes — full *deterministic* BRB with dynamic membership/reconfiguration | Yes — quorum-based, no brokers | Yes — OPODIS 2020 | Full deterministic BRB (consistency+totality = overkill) & **quorum-based → doesn't gossip-scale**; its distinctive *consensus-free dynamic reconfiguration* is **moot for us** — the blockchain already provides agreed, evolving membership (we *have* the consensus it works to avoid). Churn = a substrate concern, not a reason to adopt DBRB |
| **Byzantine update diffusion** (Malkhi, Mansour, Reiter; Malkhi, Reiter, Rodeh, Sella) | Yes — epidemic diffusion, **signature-free `≥t` acceptance rule** | Yes — replica gossip | Yes — SRDS 1999/2001; TCS 2003 | Foundational ancestor of Byzantine gossip diffusion; its distinctive trick (reject false rumors *without crypto* via `≥t` corroboration) is **moot given our blockchain PKI** (signed updates → one-copy verify) |
| **Optimal Unconditional Information Diffusion** (Malkhi, Pavlov, Sella) | Yes — **information-theoretic** (signature-free), optimal: `O(log N)` + linear in #corrupt | Yes | Yes — DISC 2001 | *Unconditional* (no-crypto) optimal diffusion; the **signature-free** property is moot given our PKI, and its linear-in-#corrupt cost is exactly the price of avoiding signatures — with a PKI that term vanishes |
| **Practical BRB on Partially Connected Networks** (Bonomi, Decouchant, Farina, Rahli, Tixeuil) | Yes — authenticated Bracha double-echo + Dolev routing (needs `2f+1`-connectivity) | Yes — point-to-point | Yes — ICDCS 2021 | Full deterministic BRB (consistency+totality = overkill) for a **constrained / partially-connected topology** (Dolev `2f+1`-disjoint-path routing) we **don't have** — we have full reachability + a chosen gossip overlay (we *sample*, not *route around unreachability*); doesn't gossip-scale |
| **Secure & Efficient Asynchronous Broadcast** (Cachin, Kursawe, Petzold, Shoup) | Yes — consistent / reliable / **atomic** / secure-causal broadcast via **threshold signatures** (`κ`-of-`n` shares), `n>3f`, static server set | Yes — among n peers, no broker overlay | Yes — CRYPTO 2001 | The *most* overkill: climbs to **atomic + causal broadcast** (ordering we explicitly don't want); **threshold crypto** (key-share setup via trusted dealer or DKG — a setup assumption, criterion-4-OK but extra) for quorum certificates we don't use; static set, quorum-based → doesn't gossip-scale |
| **Abortable Broadcast / Slot Table** (Drijvers et al., DFINITY) | Yes — bounded-memory delivery under Byzantine peers, DoS-resistant | Yes — authenticated P2P, no brokers | **No** — arXiv 2024 preprint | *Broadcast/transport primitive* (`broadcast(m)`/`deliver`, no subscription interface), not a pub/sub; sits beneath one (*confirmed 2026 review*) |
| **HyParView / Plumtree** (Leitão et al.) | No — crash/churn resilience only | Yes — gossip overlay + epidemic broadcast tree | Yes — DSN 2007 | Not Byzantine (crash/churn only); gossip overlay + epidemic broadcast tree (Plumtree is the basis of EpiSub) |
