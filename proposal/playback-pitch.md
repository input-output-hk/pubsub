# PubSub Playback Pitch (2-3 min)

## Opening — The Problem

> "Today, if you're a Cardano user, how do you find out your position is about to be liquidated? Discord. How do you learn about a governance vote? Twitter. 
>
> We're asking users to monitor social media to protect their money. And they're losing funds because of it.
>
> Cardano has no native communication layer. Every dApp builds isolated infrastructure. There's no standard way for protocols to reach users — and no way for users to broadcast intent across the ecosystem."

## The Solution

> "Cardano PubSub is the missing primitive: a decentralized message bus operated by our 3,000+ SPOs.
>
> Permissionless publishing and subscribing. Censorship resistant. Standard message formats that enable interoperability across wallets and dApps."

## Why It Matters — KPIs

> "This directly impacts our 2030 targets:
>
> - **Transactions** — PubSub enables DeFi Intents. Every fulfilled intent becomes one or more transactions. That's new volume we can't capture today.
>
> - **Governance** — Verified proposals delivered straight to wallets with one-click voting. We're projecting 15-20% improvement in DRep participation.
>
> - **Revenue** — More transactions mean more fees. But beyond that, PubSub enables an entirely new class of applications with their own revenue streams:
>   - **Premium notification services** — Protocols charging for priority alerts (liquidation warnings, whale movements, arbitrage signals)
>   - **Intent marketplaces** — Agents competing to fulfill user intents, paying fees to access order flow
>   - **Subscription-based dApps** — Real-time data feeds, portfolio alerts, governance digests
>   - **Cross-protocol coordination** — Atomic swaps, bridge notifications, multi-dApp workflows
>
> These aren't possible today because there's no messaging layer to build on."

## Why Now

> "Three reasons:
>
> 1. The DeFi Intents initiative — led by Michael Smolenski — needs a message bus. Without PubSub, intents have no delivery mechanism.
>
> 2. Nested Transactions land this year. Babel Fees become possible. But gasless trading requires users to broadcast intents somewhere.
>
> 3. Our competitors aren't waiting. Ethereum has ERC-4337, Solana has Jito. We need to close this gap."

## The Ask

> "We're requesting funding for a 4-quarter roadmap:
>
> - Q3 2026: Architecture and P2P prototype
> - Q4 2026: SDK and SPO testnet
> - Q1 2027: Mainnet launch
> - Q2 2027: Wallet integrations and developer tooling
>
> PubSub is infrastructure that unlocks an entire category of applications. It makes DeFi Intents possible, makes governance accessible, and positions Cardano to compete on user experience."

## Close

> "We have the SPO network. We have the use cases waiting. We just need the communication layer to connect them."
