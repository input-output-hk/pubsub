⚠ Closed forms, not measurements. Every value on these graphs is computed from the models' closed-form laws. The laws are leading-order approximations, validated against Monte-Carlo simulation and prototype experiments only at specific parameter points (chiefly the N = 20 000, μ = 0.2 operating points, plus a handful of elevated-μ and deep-tail cells); hop counts are asymptotic with fitted constants. Away from the validated points, the curves are extrapolation, not measured behaviour.

Raw values (table view)

Bold = best in column among visible models. Hops are asymptotic (ln H / ln branching + measured offsets) — constants are simulation-only, treat as ≈.

  • All numbers are the documented closed forms (raw laws; the retired ×1.11 tail correction is not applied). No Monte Carlo.
  • Property 6 (re-provisioning) is not an axis: raise μ and move the sliders until the P(bad) dot passes δ again.
  • Eclipse costs follow the adaptive-eclipse analyses: A = a chosen victim's mean cost (cheaper direction; M3 uses the deafen-coverage reading), B = network minimum E[min] = Σj≥1 P(D≥j)H.
  • Radar normalization ranges: P(bad) log₁₀ [10⁻⁸,1] · copies/honest [0,30] · hops [3,9] · links [0,70] · Δμ [0,0.10] · collapse [0.2,0.7] · eclipse A [0,25] · eclipse B [0,12]; all clamped, oriented outward = better.
