Cardano PubSub

A verifiable communication layer
for the Cardano ecosystem

A Cardano-anchored publish/subscribe layer for authenticated ecosystem communication: identifiable publishers, verifiable events, on-chain topic commitments, and Sybil resistance derived from main-chain stake.

Why it matters

Critical communication deserves the same guarantees as transactions

Today, ecosystem-critical messages travel over social platforms and chat servers — unauthenticated, unverifiable, and open to impersonation. PubSub extends Cardano's trust guarantees to the messages that coordinate the ecosystem.

Node operations

Emergency alerts for node operators

Node builders and maintainers — teams like IOG, Amaru, and the Cardano Foundation — reach node operators with authenticated emergency and operational alerts.

Stake pools

SPO-to-delegator communication

Stake pool operators reach their delegators through a topic that provably belongs to the pool — no spoofed announcements.

Governance

dRep-to-delegator communication

dReps share voting intent and rationale with the delegators they represent, verifiably, in the CIP-1694 governance system.

Applications

dApp-to-user announcements

dApps reach their users with critical alerts and notifications tailored to the dApp's purpose — from protocol updates to user-specific events such as open positions — published under on-chain identities with spam resisted by real stake.

Research programme

From proposed design to evidence-based architecture

The workstream moves in phases: rigorous analysis of the proposed foundations first, then an architecture built on what the evidence supports.

Jul 2023
Foundations

SecureCyclon published

Antonov & Voulgaris publish SecureCyclon at ICDCS 2023 — a Byzantine-hardened peer-sampling protocol, proposed as the decentralised foundation the PubSub workstream would build on.

Feb 2026
Phase 1 · Complete · Feb – Jul 2026

Exploratory research

Use-case and requirements analysis, cryptographic key-material study, and formal plus empirical analysis of the proposed peer-sampling foundation. The analysis surfaced silent attack vectors — adversaries can bias nodes' views of the network without triggering any of the protocol's defences — which led to the sampling layer being set aside in favour of an on-chain, list-based design.

Jul 2026
Phase 2 · Active · Jul – Sep 2026

Empirically-driven architecture

Five candidate dissemination models, from simple push to hybrid designs, analysed formally and measured empirically against a silent Byzantine adversary — so the final design is the one the data supports, not the one intuition favours. A Rust prototype node implements topics, subscriptions, publishing, and fan-out forwarding, backed by a deterministic simulation framework for controlled experiments.

Ahead
Planned

Hardening & ecosystem integration

A design proposal consolidates the analytical and experimental findings — the measured architecture, its adversary model, and positioning against existing art — published as a CIP if phase 2 time permits. Beyond it: the fee-and-incentive model that closes the remaining Byzantine gaps, real network transport in the prototype node, and a public testnet deployment. On the research side, larger networks reopen the peer-sampling question — further protocol analysis of decentralised sampling at scales where the on-chain list no longer suffices.
