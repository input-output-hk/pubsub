# PubSub - Bi-Weekly Report: 7 August - 20 August 2026

## Executive Summary

This period closed the model question. With the design target held at a bad-graph probability of 10⁻⁴, the two candidates were written up at their operating points, M3 at (13, 7) and M4 at RF = 9, M4 led six of the seven measured axes, and on 20 August the group **selected M4 as the dissemination design**. The evidence changed character along the way: the experiments stopped assuming unbounded honest resources, measuring the hash gate and the acceptance cap under both link structures, and the synthesis produced a gated M4 recommendation (K = 10, B = 500, C = 23) that measures equal or better than the ungated point on every quoted axis but latency. The CIP consolidated into a single pull request, [#164](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/164), positioned as a draft technical report, with a CPS split out and an on-chain parameter output added. Named and open: **small networks**, where the gate switches off below roughly thirty-seven participants and the agreed answer is oversaturation rather than tuning, and three recorded inconsistencies between the specification's stated rules and its specified values. The phase milestone is unchanged at mid-September.

## [epic(proto): M-model experimentation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/79)

The ungated evidence was finished first. Churn tolerance passed its test in thirty-two of thirty-four cells up to an adversarial fraction of 0.44, and the same run was the first to measure standing links (M3's busiest node holds 64 connections against M4's 36) ([PR #150](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/150)). The carried ×1.11 deep-tail correction was rejected at z = −5.7 over 370 000 pooled draws, with its retirement filed as [#165](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/165) ([PR #160](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/160)), and the coverage laws were shown to hold as μ varies from 0.20 to 0.40, licensing the re-provisioning inversions ([PR #163](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/163), [PR #167](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/167)). The two preferred operating points gained committed configurations, a measured write-up and their own churn round ([PR #162](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/162), [PR #169](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/169), [PR #176](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/176)): M3 (13, 7) at P(bad) 4.4 × 10⁻⁵ with a 2.17 % churn budget, M4 RF = 9 at 6.1 × 10⁻⁶ with 7.43 %, and every figure the documents quote now traces to a recorded cell.

The resource-bounded batch, all by Ezequiel, is the period's structural advance. E10 and E12 measured the directional hash gate: law-exact while the survivor headroom stays at or above 2, and under flooding the harm is starved honest links rather than attacker slot count, giving a cap rule of roughly twice RF ([PR #152](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/152)). E18 showed that headline does not transfer to the symmetric handshake: an empty-pool isolation channel appears instead, answered by a pool-floor rule ([PR #174](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/174)). E19 resolved what an acceptance cap means under symmetry (ADR 0042): an admissions budget on peer-initiated edges only, so a flooder can never veto a node's own selections and degree is bounded by K + C by construction ([PR #177](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/177)). E20 composed the four into the CIP-facing answer: gated M4 at K = 10, B = 500, C = 23 measures P(bad) 5.1 × 10⁻⁶ against the ungated 6.1 × 10⁻⁶ and a 7.57 % against 7.43 % churn budget at about 1 % more latency, while at equal attack surface no M3 pick count meets the 10⁻⁴ target ([PR #182](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/182)). All baseline sweeps stayed byte-identical throughout. Next: the arrival-order effect recorded as N-042, retiring the ×1.11 correction, and pricing oversaturated small topics.

## [epic(analysis): dissemination topology models](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/76)

Mauro's first contributions landed. Adaptive eclipse cost corrected the figure carried for M4: a bidirectional link is one connection and one corruption removes it, so the cost is the degree itself rather than twice it, moving M4 to second cheapest at the published points though the recommended points still favour it ([PR #140](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/140)). Re-provisioning became the sixth analysed property: M3's bandwidth lead over M4 survives at every design μ up to 0.35, and both headline points were verified, M3 (13, 7) quadrupling its μ-shift budget for 8.3 % more bandwidth and M4 RF = 9 lifting its budget from 0.209 to 0.259 for 13.6 % more ([PR #151](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/151)). An interactive closed-forms dashboard over M1 to M5 shipped and is on the website as *Compare designs* ([PR #166](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/166)).

The sessions fixed two methodological positions: candidates are compared by weighted properties with security weighted ahead of efficiency, and the closed forms and the simulations now agree within the Wilson intervals, retiring the doubt that the comparison figures were a measurement artefact. Open threads: defensible weights for the comparison (Will), the cross-partition connection probability (Ezequiel), and whether multi-topic scaling, where M4 may trail M3, joins the measured property set.

## [Pub/Sub Phase 2 — empirically-driven architecture](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/46)

**M4 is the selected dissemination design.** The 11 August comparison at the fixed 10⁻⁴ target put M4 ahead on six of seven axes, with M3 leading bandwidth by 22 %; the asymmetry is one of consequence, since at twenty-five topics the bandwidth gap (2.1 against 2.4 Mbit/s) binds nothing while the connection gap (950 against 400) is file-descriptor territory. The structural reading followed on 13 August: a bidirectional link serves both directions, so M4 has effectively twice the usable degree at the same nominal count, and the selection was made on 20 August. Symmetry's consequences were worked through in the same sessions: hash gating keeps its 1/B property only when gating on the canonical identity-sorted pair, the acceptance cap becomes the admissions budget above, and since the replication factor and the bucket size are not equally changeable after deployment the parameter discussion is reframed around headroom.

**Small networks are the named gap**, with the gate switching off below roughly thirty-seven participants for M4 and fifty-three for M3's relay kind. Topics on the order of ten participants are answered with oversaturation, letting the topology form a clique, rather than size-dependent tuning; publishing the bucket count on chain is recorded as a direction under discussion. On resilience, PubSub exists for emergencies and must keep functioning when the chain halts or forks, so two documentable heuristics were adopted for the draft: topology cadence (retained cross-epoch connections, heartbeat-driven rotation) and manual trust tables never derived from chain state. Will is asking the Cardano networking team about their original rationale for bidirectional links. Next: the CIP draft to team review, the small-topic connection-cost simulation, and the heuristics written into the CIP.

## [epic(design): solution architecture & CIP](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/91)

The motivation merged ([PR #145](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/145)): the gap, why GossipSub-style P2P alone does not close it, and the framing that audiences of hundreds of thousands are reached through roughly ten wallet backends, so the disseminating population is dominated by the roughly 3,000 SPO nodes. The specification advanced through focused merges: citations for every referenced standard, lifecycles as numbered RFC 2119 steps with a topic ending at an announced future epoch, the withdrawal delay re-justified and the pick count given one name, address resolution as an interface with the on-chain endpoint RECOMMENDED, and publisher authorisation settled with grants read at the epoch snapshot and revocations at the chain tip ([PR #155](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/155)-[#159](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/159), [#161](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/161), [#173](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/173)).

The proposal then consolidated into one pull request, [#164](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/164), positioned as a draft technical report whose `Path to Active` section inventories what blocks a buildable specification, what are priced deployment choices, and what belongs to other layers. The proof-read pass ([PR #184](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/184)) addressed thirty review notes: a CPS split out, the specification reordered to follow the architecture figure, an on-chain parameter output from which every node derives the same bucket count, serving cap and pick count, retroactive revocation, and topic protection named per size. It also recorded three open decisions the draft must settle: the bucket-count rule yields bounds near 850 to 1,000 while the proposal specifies 500, the serving cap does not follow from its rule, and the gated coverage law is absent although two MUST-derived parameters depend on it. The text was separately judged too dense; plain, human-readable prose becomes the primary artefact with dense data kept for reference. Next: settle the three decisions, apply the prose direction, and put the draft to team review, with public submission to follow.
