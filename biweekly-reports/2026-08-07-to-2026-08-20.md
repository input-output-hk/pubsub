# PubSub - Bi-Weekly Report: 7 August - 20 August 2026

## Executive Summary

This period closed the model question. With the design target held at a bad-graph probability of 10⁻⁴, the two candidates were written up at their operating points, M3 at (13, 7) and M4 at RF = 9, and M4 led six of the seven measured axes; the group then settled how to weigh them, security first and efficiency second, and on 20 August **selected M4 as the dissemination design**. The evidence behind that choice changed character: the experiments stopped assuming unbounded honest resources. The hash gate and the acceptance cap were measured under both link structures (E10, E12, E18, E19), the cap's meaning under symmetric links was resolved as an admissions budget (ADR 0042), and the synthesis (E20) produced a gated M4 recommendation, K = 10, B = 500, C = 23, that measures equal or better than the ungated operating point on every quoted axis but latency. On the analysis side the carried ×1.11 deep-tail correction was rejected at z = −5.7 over 370 000 draws, the coverage laws were shown to hold as the adversarial fraction varies, and re-provisioning and adaptive eclipse cost joined the property set. The CIP moved from a stack of section pull requests into one proposal, [PR #164](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/164), repositioned as a draft technical report, and a proof-read pass split out a CPS and introduced an on-chain parameter output from which every node derives the same admission parameters. Two things are named and open: **small networks**, where the gate switches off below roughly thirty-seven participants and the agreed answer is oversaturation rather than tuning; and three recorded inconsistencies between the specification's stated rules and its specified values, which the draft must settle before it goes to review. The phase milestone is unchanged at mid-September.

## [epic(proto): M-model experimentation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/79)

Fourteen merges. The first group finished the ungated evidence. **Churn tolerance** ([PR #150](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/150), E13) tested the reduction of downtime *p* to μ + p(1−μ) across thirty-four cells and placed the prediction inside the measurement's interval in thirty-two of them, from an adversarial fraction of 0.20 to 0.44; the same run was the first to measure standing links, reproducing the published means exactly (M4 16.00, M3 37.99, M5 33.99, M1 and M2 47.97) and adding the maxima, where M3's busiest node holds 64 connections against M4's 36. The **deep-tail correction** was measured on M4 as well as M3 ([PR #160](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/160)): pooled over 370 000 draws, two designs, two network sizes and four master seeds the factor is 0.994 ± 0.021, so the carried ×1.11 is rejected at z = −5.7 and its retirement is filed as [#165](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/165). The **coverage laws hold as μ varies** ([PR #163](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/163)): twenty-nine cells across five designs, μ from 0.20 to 0.40 and 116 000 draws land inside the Wilson interval in twenty-four, pooled measured-over-predicted 1.017 ± 0.012, which licenses inverting a law at a design μ other than 0.2, as every re-provisioning argument does. The laws' residual 2 % optimism was shown to be two opposite-sign effects rather than one ([PR #167](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/167)); at the operating points a 10⁻⁴ target reads as about 1.02 × 10⁻⁴. The **two preferred operating points** gained committed configurations ([PR #162](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/162)), a write-up of their measured 200-run cells at N = 20 000 ([PR #169](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/169)), and their own churn round ([PR #176](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/176)), closing a provenance gap where the earlier round had tested superseded parameters. M3 (13, 7): P(bad) 4.4 × 10⁻⁵, 10.40 copies per honest node, standing links 38.0 mean and 64 maximum, 5.51 hops to full coverage, churn budget 2.17 %. M4 RF = 9: 6.1 × 10⁻⁶, 13.40, 18.0 and 37, 5.00, 7.43 %. Every figure the documents quote is now sourced: the checker moved from two unsourced values to 28 checked, none mismatched.

The second group is the resource-bounded batch the previous period asked for, all by Ezequiel. **E10 and E12** ([PR #152](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/152)) measured the hash gate's coverage cost and its flooding value under the directional handshake: gated picks are law-exact while the survivor headroom r = (N−1)/(B·K) stays at or above 2, r = 1 is a fivefold cliff, gate-only selection doubles P(bad) at equal mean degree, and sizing B = (N−1)/(RF+1) restores the law. Under flooding the attacker's slot concentration lands exactly at K/B and the harm is starved honest links rather than slot count: raising the cap from 20 to 24 hands the attacker 6.83 to 7.57 slots yet converts P(good) = 0.30 into 150 good runs of 150, giving a cap-sizing rule of roughly 2·RF. **E18** ([PR #174](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/174)) was the first gated run under the symmetric handshake and found that E10's headline does not transfer: there is no r ≥ 2 plateau in this family, and an empty-pool isolation channel is real and independent of K (57 and 61 bad graphs in 8 000 at B = 250 where the matched-degree ungated twin ran none, a naive transfer underpredicting by about 430×). The design rule that came out is a pool floor, (N−1)/B ≥ ln(H/δ)/(1−μ). **E19** ([PR #177](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/177)) resolved what the acceptance cap means under symmetry, ADR 0042: an admissions budget on peer-initiated edges only, so a node's own selections can never be vetoed by a flooder and realised degree is bounded by K + C by construction; the crossing veto the pre-ADR scan measured at 0.074 per victim fell to zero, and past the pool floor a tight cap makes the bad regime worse (166 of 800 against the uncapped law's 0.148). **E20** ([PR #182](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/182)) composed the four into the CIP-facing answer with a prediction ledger whose ungated case recovers each published law. Gated M4 at K = 10, B = 500, C = 23 measures P(bad) 5.1 × 10⁻⁶ against the ungated point's 6.1 × 10⁻⁶, 13.00 copies per honest node against 13.40, churn budget 7.57 % against 7.43 %, degree at most 33 exactly, and about 1 % more mean latency. Normalised to equal attack surface no M3 pick count meets the 10⁻⁴ target, and the equal-cost alternative is feasible at 4.4 × 10⁻⁵ but roughly nine times behind gated M4. The merges that touched the node kept all seven baseline sweeps byte-identical.

Next: the arrival-order effect recorded as N-042 (the canonical order amplifies per-node tails, and the fix was parked), retiring the ×1.11 correction ([#165](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/165)), and pricing oversaturated small topics in connections per node.

## [epic(analysis): dissemination topology models](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/76)

Mauro's first contributions landed. **Adaptive eclipse cost** ([PR #140](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/140)) reuses the vertex-degree analysis and corrected the figure the backlog had carried for M4: a bidirectional link is one connection and one corruption removes it, so the cost is the degree itself rather than twice it. That moves M4 from the most eclipse-resistant model to second cheapest at the published operating points, though at the points this proposal recommends the ordering favours M4. **Re-provisioning** ([PR #151](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/151)) is the sixth analysed property: the laws inverted at design μ of 0.225, 0.25, 0.3 and 0.35. M3's bandwidth lead over M4 survives at every design point (22 % at 0.225, 7 to 15 % at 0.35, parity only near μ ≈ 0.64). It also verified both headline points. A same-budget re-split of M3 to (13, 7) quadruples its μ-shift budget from +0.004 to +0.017 for 8.3 % more bandwidth; M4 at RF = 9 raises its budget from 0.209 to 0.259 for 13.6 % more. Robustness is bought in state, 21 to 50 % more standing links at a design μ of 0.35. An **interactive closed-forms dashboard** ([PR #166](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/166)) ports the M1 to M5 closed forms to a dependency-free page: a radar over eight properties, sliders for μ, N, δ and each model's parameters with live P(bad) against the target, a 42-of-42 self-test, and re-provisioning inversions that reproduce the documents. It is on the website as *Compare designs*.

Two methodological positions were fixed in the sessions. The comparison has an **ordering**: candidates are compared by weighted properties with security weighted ahead of efficiency, and Will takes the follow-up of choosing weights that can be defended. And the **instrument and the laws agree**: after a week and a half of near-continuous sweeps the closed forms and the simulations line up within the Wilson intervals once residual noise and bias are removed, which retires the doubt that the comparison figures might be a measurement artefact. Mauro raised whether the property set is complete, naming multi-topic scaling as an unmeasured axis on which M4 may come out behind M3; that stays open. Ezequiel took an analytical follow-up on partitions: the probability that nodes on opposite sides of a chain fork fail to connect at all, and how many random connections make that negligible.

Next: defensible weights for the comparison, the cross-partition connection probability, and a ruling on multi-topic scaling as a measured property.

## [Pub/Sub Phase 2 — empirically-driven architecture](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/46)

**M4 is the selected dissemination design.** The decision came in three steps. On 11 August the comparison at a fixed target of 10⁻⁴ put M4 ahead on six of seven axes: connections held (18 against 38 on average, 37 against 64 at the busiest node), churn tolerance (7.4 % against 2.2 % of the honest population offline), hops to the last subscriber, margin below the failure target, and adaptive eclipse cost, with M3 leading bandwidth by 22 %. The asymmetry is one of consequence rather than count: at twenty-five topics the bandwidth difference is 2.1 against 2.4 Mbit/s, which binds nothing, while the connection difference is 950 against 400, which is file-descriptor territory. On 13 August the group agreed the weighting and worked through why the symmetric model behaves better than first sketched: a bidirectional link serves both directions, so M4 has effectively twice the usable degree of M3's relay arrangement at the same nominal count, which is also why it degrades more gracefully when bidirectional links are lost. On 20 August M4 was selected over M3 and M5, confirming the direction the CIP draft already takes.

Three consequences of symmetry were worked through. **Hash gating loses the 1/B property** when a pair can be admitted from either side; the repair is to gate on the canonical, identity-sorted pair, which restores the density but requires the bucket count to be derived to preserve pool headroom rather than chosen independently. **Acceptance caps mean something different**, since an incoming request that yields a bidirectional link is not the same object as an incoming-only one; ADR 0042 settled this as the admissions budget above. And **parameters are not equally changeable after deployment**: the replication factor and the bucket size are not interchangeable dials once a network is live, which reframes the parameter discussion around headroom, how far a network can grow and how far the adversarial fraction can drift before the configuration must be revised.

**Small networks are the named gap.** The gate switches off once the bucket count cannot reach two, below roughly four times the pick count: about thirty-seven participants for M4 at a pick count of nine, about fifty-three for M3's relay kind at thirteen, so the symmetric model reaches further down a small topic rather than less far. Below that the serving cap is the only defence against a flooder, and Sybil resistance is structurally harder because an attacker's identities are a larger fraction of a smaller population. The agreed answer for topics on the order of ten participants is oversaturation, letting the topology form a clique, rather than size-dependent parameter tuning, since the structure moves away from a clique of its own accord as a topic grows; simulations are still needed to price this in connections per node. Publishing the bucket count on chain, potentially per topic, was discussed as the route to revising it as a population fluctuates, and is recorded as a direction under discussion rather than a settled mechanism.

**Chain forks and the case for not depending on the chain.** A fork makes nodes compute different epoch topologies from different views and splits the overlay along the same line, and PubSub exists for emergencies, so it has to keep functioning when the chain halts or forks. Two documentable heuristics came out: topology cadence, letting nodes retain connections from previous epochs and rotate intra-epoch under a fresh seed via heartbeats, and manual trust tables of known peers whose links were never derived from chain state. Epoch randomness has an inherent tension of its own, unbiased against stable, and a predictable beacon gives an adversary advance knowledge of the next topology. Will is asking the Cardano networking team why they chose bidirectional links, in case that choice answers a failure this project has not yet named.

Next: the CIP draft to team review, the small-topic connection-cost simulation, and the heartbeat and manual-trust heuristics written into the CIP.

## [epic(design): solution architecture & CIP](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/91)

The **motivation merged** ([PR #145](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/145), closing [#105](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/105)): the gap (ecosystem messaging runs on operator-controlled channels beneath a BFT chain), why GossipSub-style P2P alone does not close it (eclipse resistance bottoms out on a discovery layer that admits free identities), the four scenarios by participant count, and five solution requirements. Its load-bearing framing is that audiences of hundreds of thousands are reached through roughly ten wallet backends, so the disseminating population is in the thousands and dominated by the roughly 3,000 SPO nodes. The specification then advanced through a stack of focused merges: citations for every protocol and standard referenced ([PR #156](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/156)); the three security properties as a list ([PR #155](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/155)); node and topic lifecycles as numbered steps with RFC 2119 constraints, surfacing four implicit MUSTs and fixing a correctness point where a topic ends at an announced future epoch rather than on entry removal, the same shape as pool retirement ([PR #158](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/158)); the withdrawal delay justified by containment and turnover instead of unenforceable evidence-after-retirement, and one name, *k*, for the pick count ([PR #157](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/157)); address resolution as an interface with four requirements and the on-chain endpoint RECOMMENDED with its cost stated, every participant's address public and permanent ([PR #159](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/159)); the serving-cap figure dropped in favour of the prose that already carried its evidence ([PR #161](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/161)); and the publisher-authorisation position settled, grants at the epoch snapshot and revocations at the chain tip once rollback-safe ([PR #173](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/173), answering [#133](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/133)).

**The proposal is one pull request and a draft technical report.** [PR #164](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/164), opened 11 August, replaces the stacked rationale and specification PRs with a single diff against `main`, and the group agreed the document should say plainly what it is: a report of what the evidence establishes with the open questions visible, rather than a finished design proposal. The `Path to Active` section carries that inventory in three groups: what blocks a buildable specification, what are deployment choices the analysis prices rather than makes, and what belongs to layers this proposal does not define. First review comments arrived on 13 August, and the **proof-read pass** ([PR #184](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/184)) addressed thirty review notes: a CPS is split out to `docs/cps/README.md` and the Motivation names the scenario the proposal is sized for; the Specification is reordered to follow the architecture figure with Terminology and CDDL in an appendix; a new on-chain **parameter output** carries the adversarial fraction, failure target, honest downtime rate, adversarial identity count and epoch length so every node derives the same bucket count, serving cap and pick count; revocation becomes retroactive; identity gains proof of possession and a display encoding; and topic protection is named per size, gate for large, deposit plus near-complete mesh for small, nothing measured for the few hundred in between. Two figures were corrected (the pool bound is 846, and a node's own isolation risk sits about four orders below the network figure, not nine or ten) and all 133 internal references resolve. The pass also recorded **three open decisions** the draft must settle: the stated rule yields bucket-count bounds of 846, 999 and about 730 while the proposal specifies 500; the specified serving cap does not follow from its rule; and the gated coverage law is absent although two MUST-derived parameters depend on it. Separately, the specification and rationale text was found too dense to read comfortably; the agreed direction is plain, human-readable prose as the primary artefact with dense data and detailed results kept separately for reference. The identity documentation is to be reviewed for refactoring.

Next: settle the three open decisions, apply the prose direction, and put the draft to the team for review as a pull request, with public submission to follow.
