## [epic(design): solution architecture & CIP](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/91)

Two pull requests finished the draft. [PR #195](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/195) restructured it: the Specification is written top-down for the single selected design, the Rationale leads with the symmetric link and hands the five-model evaluation to a companion `design-comparison.md`, figures and tables are renumbered with rebuilt indices, and a reader audit against a Cardano implementor persona ([PR #196](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/196)) added first-use glosses wherever a symbol, model name or normalisation was used before it was defined. [PR #197](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/197) then corrected the document against its own data: the M2 flooding result is attributed to B = 125, Figure 5's 23 initial coverage cells are distinguished from the two deep-tail reruns, and the radar comparison is described as it reads, with M4 improving three axes over M5 and tying on hops. The provisional handshake binding and epoch-schedule blockers now appear at the definitions they qualify rather than only in *Path to Active*.

The merge of [PR #164](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/164) on 14 September put the whole proposal, specification, evidence and design comparison, on `main`, and the epic closed on 16 September.

## [Pub/Sub Phase 2 — empirically-driven architecture](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/46)

The CPS and CIP were submitted to `cardano-foundation/CIPs` on 16 September, problem statement first: [CPS #1270](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/CIPs/pull/1270) sets out four use cases, six goals and the open questions on adversarial participation, delivery targets, topic populations, small topics, participation cost and behaviour during a chain halt or fork, and [CIP #1271](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/CIPs/pull/1271) proposes the protocol as its solution. Both headers were reordered to satisfy the repository's validators, and the rendered links were anchored to the submission branches so reviewers follow revisions in place. Feedback is invited on two points:

- the relationship to CIP-0137, which *Path to Active* records as unresolved and wants input from its authors on;
- the deployment parameter profile, meaning the bucket count, pick count and serving cap, together with the small-topic case, where populations of tens fall outside the current analysis.

The public material was brought in line with the submission ([PR #198](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/198) to [PR #202](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/202)). The site's research timeline now carries the September publication, the review running through October and the October to November approval target, and states M4 as the selected design; the README says the same and lists the CPS alongside the CIP.
