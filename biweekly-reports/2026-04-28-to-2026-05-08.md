# PubSub - Bi-Weekly Report: 28 Apr - 8 May 2026

## Executive Summary

This period moved PubSub from broad research discussion toward a concrete delivery shape. The team narrowed scope toward unidirectional notification-style use cases, advanced trusted relay / golden tier analysis, drafted staged architecture options, expanded actor/use-case coverage, and identified SecureCyclon analysis plus prototype work as the next load-bearing steps.

---

## [Golden tier / trusted relay partitioning](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/34)

Initial golden-tier analysis for RandCast and RingCast was completed in [`formal_spec/hybrid_dissemination/partitioning/golden_tier/`](../formal_spec/hybrid_dissemination/partitioning/golden_tier/), including commit [`ee04428`](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/commit/ee04428). Follow-up discussions with Ezequiel, Denis, and Spyros clarified that trusted relays can improve delivery paths, but the quantitative recommendation still needs to account for SecureCyclon sampling bias and relay-source reliability assumptions.

Next: fold SecureCyclon assumptions and relay topology into the dissemination recommendation.

---

## [Actor & use case analysis](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/27)

The actor/use-case analysis was extended with Use Case-9 to 13 on [`origin/docs/use-case-analysis`](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/blob/main/docs/actor-use-case-analysis.md), covering Sundae, Hydra/Gummiworm, and multi-sig coordination cases beyond the original notification set. This sharpened the split between notification, request/reply, many-to-few, and small-group coordination patterns.

Next: validate the key assumptions with Sundae, Hydra/Gummiworm, wallet backends, and other relevant actors before turning the analysis into product requirements.

---

## [Architecture building blocks & candidate spec](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/9)

A staged architecture synthesis and two protocol-extension proposals were drafted on [`origin/design-explorations`](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/33): owner-attested relay tier and local mutual-trust relays. The design work connects the use-case findings to an incremental delivery path where direct relay delivery can ship before the full gossip/security analysis converges.

Next: turn the staged design into a sharper candidate specification with explicit interfaces, deployment assumptions, and acceptance criteria.

---

## [Full-stack algorithmic specification](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/25)

The team started mapping the Cardano architecture, SecureCyclon, and Vicinity-style algorithms into a common peer-to-peer interface with setup, selection, send, and receive phases. The discussion also identified uniform peer sampling from SecureCyclon as a key precondition for the higher-layer security analysis.

Next: write pseudocode for peer sampling, navigation, and dissemination, including how descriptors are protected and transferred across layers.

---

## [Cryptographic authentication & key material analysis](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/20)

Scope discussions identified SPO, dRep, DApp, and relay authentication as active design dependencies, with SPO credential reuse remaining the base case to resolve first. The trusted-relay and UC-1 design threads both rely on making this key-material story explicit; issue [#21](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/21) tracks the Cardano credential-reuse subtask.

Next: produce the SPO credential recommendation: which existing Cardano keys can be reused safely, which should be derived, and which require separate PubSub keys.

---

## [Product requirements & MVP use case selection](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/11)

The team narrowed immediate scope toward simpler unidirectional notification-style use cases and deprioritised bidirectional multi-sig-style flows for the current delivery path. Candidate priority scenarios now include IOG alerts to SPOs, dApps to users, SPOs to delegators, dReps to delegators, security pings, data availability, and off-chain signature broadcasting; issue [#12](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/12) tracks the MVP selection work.

Next: convert the narrowed scope into explicit MVP requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and stakeholder-validation questions.
