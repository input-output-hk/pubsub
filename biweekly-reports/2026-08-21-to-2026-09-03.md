## [epic(proto): M-model experimentation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/79)

The instrument pass chartered at the E20 merge landed ([PR #188](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/188)). N-042 was a defect in the simulator rather than in the designs: the wavefront's canonical delivery sort made every victim process its arrivals in the same global sender order, so a low-ranked dialler won every admission race population-wide. Class-level measurands stayed exact, which is why the E12, E18 and E19 predictions all matched, but per-node tail events built from many dial outcomes were amplified by orders of magnitude against the independent orders a real network approximates. ADR 0044 keys each recipient's order on a hash of the run seed with the recipient and sender identities, so the orders decorrelate by construction while the run stays a pure function of configuration and seeds. Both frozen pre-registrations passed: the seed-1148 cell reads 80 bad graphs in 400 against the independent-order form's 84.8, where the canonical order had measured 188. Run rows also gained the two stranding directions of the formal severity tables, counted independently with the overlap recoverable. All seven baseline sweeps were re-run and every measurand of record is value-identical; the one anticipated movement is send attribution under same-wave origin ties, bounded per slice against totals orders of magnitude larger. Next: evaluating GossipSub's scoring function against the project's named problems, and pricing oversaturated small topics.

## [epic(analysis): dissemination topology models](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/76)

Operating points are no longer selected at the bare criterion. A point is admissible only if its coverage law holds P(bad) at or below 10⁻⁴ across effective adversarial fractions up to μ + 0.016, a margin read from WAN-realistic disturbance rather than tuned to the selections ([PR #187](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/187)). The selected points are M1 F = 25, M2 RF = 25, M3 (13, 7), M4 RF = 9 and M5 (9, 8), the last three churn-validated at exactly those parameters, and the comparison document was rewritten in Simplified Technical English with a reading key on each table. One caveat is on record: pooled across churn rounds M3's law reads slightly optimistic, mechanism unidentified and direction conservative, and if it resolves into a real correction then M3's admissibility at (13, 7) is the first thing to re-read.

The ×1.11 deep-tail correction is retired, closing [#165](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/165) ([PR #189](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/189)). The dedicated measurement reads the factor at 0.994 ± 0.021 over 370,000 fresh draws and the cell it was originally measured on does not reproduce. It flipped exactly one decision, so M4 at μ_design = 0.35 stands at RF = 11 rather than 12 and the frontier verdict's M3 lead there settles at about 7 %. Validity caveats were replaced rather than deleted: the tail at the operating points is still labelled law-read, and every rewritten note cites the measurement including its own limit.

## [Pub/Sub Phase 2 — empirically-driven architecture](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/46)

The review's design questions were worked through on 1 September. **The registry stays, and the CIP will say it is a choice.** The protocol serves use cases with no existing credential and a bespoke identity system per use case is not a design; an inherited stake root has economics sized for consensus rather than for this layer's guarantees; and the deposit is a v1 foundation that can evolve toward decay or slashing, which borrowed credentials cannot. **The stake-pool figures were replaced with measured ones:** registrations have never exceeded 2,696 against the 3,000 to 4,000 the draft cited, so the number now bounds the population from above, and the 20,000-node evaluation point stays justified as headroom.

**Chain dependencies got boundaries**, since the chain is the thing most likely to be degraded in the scenario the protocol exists for. The randomness beacon is stated as not necessarily Cardano-origin, operator-configured trusted links enter the CIP as the fork-and-halt mitigation, and Fireflies gets a feasibility look as a registry-independent peer-sampling layer. **Incentives are stated as assumptions**, with the deposit reclaimable and rational silence folded into the adversarial fraction rather than pretending to be detectable. **The eligible-set hit-list is confirmed real:** with endpoints published and the gate public, an adversary who can link a publisher to its node identity can compute the roughly forty eligible peers and silence the topic, so the CIP states the attack and describes the private-relay endpoint pattern alongside the on-chain option. Scoring functions are referenced as a future evaluation, since global cross-topic scoring admits the mute-one-topic attack and heuristics add attack surface against a criterion that is already checkable.

## [epic(design): solution architecture & CIP](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/91)

The plain-prose direction was carried through the whole document. The Specification now states each registration rule once, specifies the topic registry before the node registry, adds per-topic epoch length as a fifth arrangement, gives the handshake and joining sequences their own figures, and defines the colliding vocabulary where the text first leans on it. The Rationale was split into three sections, each of the five candidate designs gained its own figure, the derived and uncited columns came out of the evidence tables, and twenty-nine defensive or self-praising phrases went with them. The three recorded inconsistencies are answered: the coverage law is stated in the Specification with the gated and ungated forms named as one family, the B = 500 against 512 mismatch surfaces in *Limits* as an instance of the extrapolation the document already carries, and the serving cap is rewritten against its rule. The five specification sub-issues closed on 31 August ([#103](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/103), [#131](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/131)-[#134](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/134)).

Two stacked pull requests added material rather than compressing it. [PR #190](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/190) pins nine Specification rules to the block of `pubsub-node` that decides each, by permalink at a fixed commit, on the line that a rule about behaviour is settled by reading the code where a rule fixing a byte string is not; building it surfaced that the node's encodings diverge on purpose and that the refused-dial retry seam was stated twice and inconsistently. [PR #193](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/193) answers the first question a networking reader asks, why this traffic cannot ride the node-to-node layer Cardano already runs:

- **Its membership is the wrong set.** No on-chain registration puts a wallet backend, a dApp service or a dRep into it.
- **Its guarantee does not transfer.** Chain diffusion is self-checking against withholding, where a message that never arrives is indistinguishable from one that was never sent.
- **It would share fate with what it protects.** The channel that warns operators about a fault in their node would run on the links consensus depends on.

The 2 September fixes priced the requirements that were doing quantitative work in absolute words. The grinding requirement states its arithmetic, a source biasable by *b* bits weakens the gate by 2^*b*, so a candidate randomness source needs a stated bias tolerance. The snapshot rule gained the confirmation depth that revocation already had, since every agreement argument rests on the cutoff not moving under a rollback. The equivocation proof pair became the forwarding ceiling as well as the evidence, since nothing had stopped a compromised key minting unlimited payloads under one sequence number. Next: the draft to internal team review, then public submission.
