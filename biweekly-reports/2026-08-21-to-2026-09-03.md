## [epic(proto): M-model experimentation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/79)

The instrument pass chartered at the E20 merge landed ([PR #188](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/188)). N-042 was a defect in the simulator, not in the designs: the wavefront's canonical delivery sort made every victim process its intra-wave arrivals in the same global sender order, so a low-ranked dialler won every admission race population-wide. Class-level measurands were exact under that order, which is why every prediction in E12, E18 and E19 matched, but per-node tail events built from many dial outcomes were amplified by orders of magnitude against the independent orders a real network approximates. The fix (ADR 0044) keys each recipient's intra-wave order on a hash of the run seed with the recipient and sender identities, decorrelating the orders by construction while the run stays a pure function of configuration and seeds. Both frozen pre-registrations passed: the seed-1148 cell reads 80 bad graphs in 400 against the independent-order form's 84.8 (z = −0.59) where the canonical order had measured 188, and the E19 ordered flooder reads 400 good in 400 against 386, with its race and dial columns identical run for run, so the budget refuses the same load under both orders and only the per-node allocation moved.

The parked failure-severity change came in the same batch. Run rows now carry the two stranding directions of the formal severity tables, the vertices the largest component cannot reach and the vertices that cannot reach it, computed in the existing condensation pass and counted independently, with the convention difference stated at every surface so the overlap is recoverable rather than silently double-counted. All seven baseline sweeps were then re-run under the new generation and every measurand of record is value-identical: good counts, topology verdicts, coverage, receipts, depth, and the degree histograms. The one anticipated movement is send attribution under same-wave origin ties, bounded per slice against totals three to five orders larger, and the symmetric sweep is provably exempt because in a symmetric graph every in-neighbour is also an out-neighbour, so whichever tied sender wins the origin slot the excluded forward already holds the message. Next: evaluating GossipSub's scoring function against the project's named problems, and pricing oversaturated small topics.

## [epic(analysis): dissemination topology models](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/76)

Operating points are no longer selected at the bare criterion. A point is admissible if, and only if, its coverage law holds P(bad) at or below 10⁻⁴ across effective adversarial fractions up to μ + 0.016, the margin read from WAN-realistic disturbance, roughly 1 % send loss with churn of the same order, and anchored there rather than tuned to the selections; within a model the choice is by dominance over messages per message and mean standing links ([PR #187](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/187)). The selected points are M1 F = 25, M2 RF = 25, M3 (13, 7), M4 RF = 9 and M5 (9, 8), with the M3, M4 and M5 laws churn-validated at exactly those parameters. The comparison document was rewritten in Simplified Technical English with a reading key on each of the three comparison tables, and a per-model transmission-unreliability analysis was added as the prerequisite. One caveat is on record: pooled across churn rounds M3's law reads slightly optimistic (Stouffer +2.41, mechanism unidentified, direction conservative), and if that resolves into a real correction, M3's admissibility at (13, 7) is the first thing to re-read.

The ×1.11 deep-tail correction is retired, closing [#165](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/165) ([PR #189](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/189)). The dedicated measurement reads the small-component factor at 0.994 ± 0.021 over 370,000 fresh draws, and the cell it was originally measured on does not reproduce. It flipped exactly one decision, so M4 at μ_design = 0.35 stands at RF = 11 rather than 12, M3 (17, 7) regains its raw margin at μ_design = 0.3, and the frontier verdict's M3 lead at 0.35 settles at about 7 % where the old range spanned the retired reading. The editorial rule agreed in the issue thread was followed throughout: validity caveats are replaced, not deleted, so the tail at the operating points is still labelled law-read rather than directly measured, and every rewritten note cites the measurement including its own limit, that the factor is measured two decades above the operating tail.

## [Pub/Sub Phase 2 — empirically-driven architecture](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/46)

The review's design questions were worked through on 1 September. **The registry stays, and the CIP will say it is a choice.** The alert scenario's audience holds a stake-backed credential already, so the review asked why a deposit registry rather than the SPO root of trust. Three answers held: the protocol serves use cases with no existing credential and a bespoke identity system per use case is not a design; an inherited stake root has economics sized for consensus rather than for this layer's guarantees; and the deposit is a v1 foundation that can evolve toward decay or slashing, which borrowed credentials cannot. The CIP-0137 criterion is reworded from interoperability to alternatives-or-complements.

**The stake-pool figures were replaced with measured ones.** Registrations have never exceeded 2,696 (Blockfrost, epochs 210 to 646), against the 3,000 to 4,000 the draft cited, and the pools holding 99 % of stake number about 900. The number now bounds the population from above rather than describing its order, and the 20,000-node evaluation point stays justified as headroom.

**Chain dependencies got boundaries**, since the chain is the thing most likely to be degraded in the scenario the protocol exists for. The randomness beacon is stated as not necessarily Cardano-origin, any public agreed source qualifies; operator-configured trusted links enter the CIP as the fork-and-halt mitigation, following the SPO private-relay pattern; and Fireflies gets a feasibility look as a registry-independent peer-sampling layer, its Sybil-resistance unverified. Emergency and normal operation are framed separately.

**Incentives are stated as assumptions, not solved.** The deposit is a registration deposit, reclaimable, with no slashing and no decay, and the CIP says so plainly. Rational silence folds into the adversarial fraction rather than pretending to be detectable, since burst attacks resist classification even with a perfect record of who relayed what. Decaying deposits, continuous stake redistribution and threshold-signature participation proofs were walked through and parked as beyond this proposal. **The eligible-set hit-list is confirmed real:** with endpoints published and the gate public, an adversary who can link a publisher to its node identity can compute the roughly forty eligible peers and silence the topic, so the CIP states the attack and describes the private-relay endpoint pattern alongside the on-chain option. Scoring functions are referenced as a future evaluation: the formal record behind them is shaky, global cross-topic scoring admits the mute-one-topic attack, and heuristics add attack surface against a design whose strongly-connected-graph criterion is checkable.

## [epic(design): solution architecture & CIP](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/91)

The plain-prose direction agreed at the end of the last period was carried through the whole document. The Specification pass stated each registration rule once and let the lifecycle be the only account of it, specified the topic registry before the node registry, added per-topic epoch length as a fifth arrangement, gave the handshake and the joining sequence their own figures, and defined the colliding vocabulary where the text first leans on it: muting, retention counted in epochs, churn. The Rationale pass split the largest container into three sections, gave each of the five candidate designs its own figure, led every evidence subsection with its visual, cut the derived and uncited columns out of the evidence tables, and removed twenty-nine defensive or self-praising phrases. The closing pass on 31 August reworked the three Rationale figures so each carries its own reading, and rewrote *Path to Active* to state its criteria instead of re-arguing them. The five specification sub-issues closed on 31 August ([#103](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/103), [#131](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/131)-[#134](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/issues/134)).

The three recorded inconsistencies are now answered. The coverage law is stated in the Specification and joins the Terminology table, with the gated and ungated forms named as one family, so the two MUST-derived parameters that depend on it have something to derive from. The B = 500 against the rule's 512 band is no longer silent: it surfaces in *Limits of this evidence* as an instance of the extrapolation the document already carries. The serving cap was rewritten in plainer and shorter prose against the rule it comes from, with its callout no longer restating its own subsection.

Two stacked pull requests added material rather than compressing it. [PR #190](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/190) pins nine Specification rules to the block of `pubsub-node` that decides each, by permalink at a fixed commit so the links survive a move to the Cardano Foundation repository. Nine and not more, on a deliberate line: a rule about behaviour is settled by reading the code that decides it, where a rule fixing a byte string is not, so the encoding rules are left out. Building the map surfaced that the node's encodings diverge from the document on purpose (the domain tags are deferred under N-016, and ADR 0031 §5 sets the tag convention while there are no deployed peers to stay compatible with), and that the refused-dial retry seam was stated twice and inconsistently, now narrowed to what is genuinely open. [PR #193](https://github.com/input-output-hk/pubsub/pull/193) answers the first question a networking reader asks, why this traffic cannot ride the node-to-node layer Cardano already runs, with three reasons:

- **Its membership is the wrong set.** No on-chain registration puts a wallet backend, a dApp service or a dRep into it, and those are how three of the four scenarios reach their audience.
- **Its guarantee does not transfer.** Chain diffusion is self-checking against withholding, since blocks are expected at a known rate; a message that never arrives is indistinguishable from a message that was never sent.
- **It would share fate with what it protects.** The channel that warns operators about a fault in their node would be running on the links consensus depends on.

The 2 September fixes priced the requirements that were doing quantitative work in absolute words. The grinding requirement now states its arithmetic, a randomness source biasable by *b* bits weakens the gate by 2^*b* and nine bits defeat the widest band, so a candidate source needs a stated bias tolerance. The snapshot rule gained the confirmation depth that revocation already had, since every agreement argument rests on the cutoff not moving under a rollback. The equivocation proof pair became the forwarding ceiling as well as the evidence: both messages of a conflicting pair must propagate for the fault to be detectable, but nothing had bounded it at two, so a compromised key could mint unlimited payloads under one sequence number. Rate limiting joined the named seams. Next: the draft to internal team review, then public submission.
